## A little about me

I am **Kim Jerald Sialana**, a third-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student focused on building clear, responsive, and visually engaging front-end experiences. I enjoy combining development and design to turn ideas into interfaces that feel useful, intentional, and easy to understand.

I am also exploring how AI tools can support a more thoughtful development workflow—from research and prototyping to debugging, documentation, and iteration—while keeping human judgment, design quality, and maintainability at the center.

> **Design-minded developer. Front-end focused. Always learning.**
